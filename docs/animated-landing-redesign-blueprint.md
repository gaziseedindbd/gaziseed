# Animated Landing Page — Redesign Blueprint and Exact Copy

Campaign example: `/animated-landing/ADFDF`. These are suggested production-ready CMS values; product variety, documented agronomic claims, prices and current stock must be verified against the linked product before publishing. Copy is in English, Bengali and Hindi. Never overwrite an operator's valid campaign copy automatically.

## Desktop structure
1. Compact sticky campaign header: brand, one genuine-seed trust label, Order Now.
2. Two-column hero: product and promise / original product photograph; on mobile stack headline, image, benefits and CTA.
3. Problem → Solution → Our Seeds story (each one short paragraph).
4. Benefits: 2 cards across small screens, five cards on wide desktop.
5. Growing guide: five steps; two-column cards on tablet, stack on compact mobile.
6. Package selection + order form: responsive two-column desktop; one column mobile, explicit selected-state and total.
7. Optional real testimonials, four trust highlights, closing CTA. Do not fabricate reviews or results.
